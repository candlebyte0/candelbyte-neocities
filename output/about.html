an introduction

it's wonderful to have you here. my name is gabbie (or gabrielle if you'd like), and i've decided to make a website in lieu of a social media page. i don't have much on here yet, but i'm excited to have an excuse to write more, and to be able to share it with others. i hope you'll stick around!

academic interests

i'm a college student and general lover of academia. i'm majoring in philosophy (partially because it has such a broad scope), but i also love anthropology, linguistics, politics, history, and more. beyond those social-science/humanities interests i do love learning about math and computer science, but i know far less about those than other topics. my dream job is college professor, but i'd love to be any kind of teacher.

hobbies and fun

beyond those interests, i also love lots of things outside of academia. i love games, both digital and analog. my favorite games from each category are Minecraft and Magic: the Gathering, both of which i've played since elementary school and have a probably excessive amount of brainspace devoted to. programming is also much more of a hobby than an academic interest for me.
